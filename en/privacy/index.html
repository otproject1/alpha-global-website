Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Controller

The controller within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is ALPHA GLOBAL Sp. z o.o., Warsaw, Poland (Alpha Global EU).
Email: info@alpha-global.eu
Website: www.alpha-global.eu

We have not appointed a data protection officer because we are not required to do so. For any data protection questions, please contact us at the email address above.

2. In brief

This website sets no cookies and uses no tracking or analytics services, advertising or social media plugins. We process personal data only when you visit the site (technical server data) or contact us yourself.

3. Hosting and server log files

This website is delivered through Cloudflare Pages (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open the site, the hosting provider processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, the date and time of the request, the requested page, the amount of data transferred, and the browser and operating system type.

Purpose: delivering the website, ensuring stability and security, defending against attacks. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure and functioning website). Cloudflare processes data on our behalf under a data processing agreement. Transfers to the USA rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

4. Fonts

For a consistent appearance we load fonts from Bunny Fonts (BunnyWay d.o.o., Slovenia, EU). Your IP address is transmitted to the Bunny Fonts server so that the font files can be delivered. According to its own statements, Bunny Fonts sets no cookies and creates no user profiles. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (consistent and appealing presentation).

5. Contacting us

Contact form and email. If you contact us via the form or by email, we process your name, email address, your company (if given) and the content of your message. We use this data solely to answer your enquiry and to prepare possible cooperation. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering business enquiries).

WhatsApp. The website contains only a link to WhatsApp. No data is transmitted to WhatsApp as long as you do not click the link. If you click it, you leave our website and WhatsApp's privacy terms apply (WhatsApp Ireland Ltd.). If you write to us there, we process your message and phone number to answer your enquiry.

Storage period. We keep your enquiry while it is being handled and beyond that only where statutory retention duties (for example for business correspondence) apply or where needed to defend legal claims. After that, the data is deleted.

6. Cookies and local storage

We do not set cookies on this website and do not store information on your device beyond what is technically necessary. We therefore do not need a cookie banner. Should we use analytics or marketing services in the future, we will do so only with your explicit consent (Art. 173 of the Polish Telecommunications Law, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and will update this policy.

7. Recipients

Recipients of your data are our hosting provider (Cloudflare), our email provider where you write to us by email or form, and Bunny Fonts (see above). We do not pass data to other third parties unless legally obliged. Your data is not sold.

8. Your rights

You have the right at any time to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. To do so, contact info@alpha-global.eu.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland, uodo.gov.pl. You may also contact the authority at your place of residence or work.

9. Providing data, automated decisions

Providing your data in the contact form is voluntary, but without your name and email address we cannot answer your enquiry. There is no automated decision-making or profiling.

10. External links

This website contains links to external sites (for example WhatsApp). The respective operators are responsible for their content and privacy practices.

11. Changes

We update this policy when the website or the legal situation changes. The version published here applies.
